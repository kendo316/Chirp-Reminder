Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you cover?

We currently serve Missouri, focusing on the Columbia, Kansas City, and Pacific/St. Louis metro areas. We plan to expand coverage over time.

Where does the bird data come from?

We use eBird, a real-time bird observation database managed by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. Data is based on actual sightings reported by birders in your area over the past 7 days.

When do emails arrive?

Digests are sent every Monday morning around 6:00 AM Central Time, so they're ready when you wake up to start your week.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free. We built this because we love birds and wanted to help fellow birders. No ads, no upsells.

How do I unsubscribe?

Every email includes an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you're removed immediately. No questions asked.

Can I change my zip code?

Currently, you can unsubscribe and re-sign up with your new zip code. We're working on a profile management feature.
